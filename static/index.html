A multi-agent pipeline that analyzes long-form video and identifies clips with viral potential. Five AI "viewer" agents each score segments from their own perspective — hook strength, emotional intensity, quotability, narrative structure, and trend potential. Clips that multiple agents agree on rise to the top. Then the Edit Agent trims and captions them, and the Packaging Agent generates titles, thumbnails, and descriptions.
